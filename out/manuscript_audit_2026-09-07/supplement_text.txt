P56: Figure 1. ROC curves and score distribution for the automatic integer score. Left panel: Receiver operating characteristic (ROC) curves for the automatic integer score (red, AUC=0.553) and the logistic probability benchmark (blue, AUC=0.588), compared to the random baseline (grey dashed line). Right panel: Distribution of clinical integer scores by thrombophilia test outcome (purple: negative result, orange: positive result). The vertical dashed line indicates the sensitivity-safe threshold of ≥3 points, selected to achieve a minimum sensitivity of 90%. The substantial overlap between the two distributions reflects the modest overall discrimination of the score. AUC: area under the receiver operating characteristic curve.
